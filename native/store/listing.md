## Description
SNOWD connects people who need snow cleared with local shovelers nearby.

NEED SNOW CLEARED?
• Find shovelers who work in your area and see their prices up front
• Book your driveway, walkway or sidewalk in a few taps
• Follow the job with live updates and chat with your shoveler
• Get a photo when the job is done, then rate it

CLEAR SNOW FOR MONEY?
• Set your own prices for driveways, walkways and sidewalks
• Choose the cities you work in
• Get notified the moment a neighbour needs a hand
• Get paid per job, by card or cash

BUILT ON TRUST
Members verify their government ID, and every job is rated, so you know who's coming to your door. Shovelers' home addresses are never shown to customers.

Questions? Reach us at support@snowd.ca.
